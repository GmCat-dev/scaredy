How to use Scaredy GmCat

Six small steps. About two minutes. Nothing gets stored.

  1. Pick your filesOpen scaredy.gmcat.io and tap Pick files, or drop them on the box. Pick one or many. They stay right on your device.
  2. Choose how scared you areThey're listed most secure first. Direct goes straight between your browsers. Encrypted relay works on any network and hides your internet addresses from each other. Short link gives you a link that's easy to type or say; its key passes through our server while you connect. Whichever you pick, nothing is stored.
  3. Make your private linkTap Make a private link. With Direct and Encrypted relay the secret key is part of the link itself (the bit after the "#"), so no server, ours included, can open your files. While you wait you can still add files (tap Add more files or drop them anywhere on the page) or remove them with ✕; the other person's list updates straight away.
  4. Send the linkTap Copy link and paste it into a message, or show the QR code. Send it only to the person it's for: anyone with the whole link can get the file while you're sharing.
  5. Keep your page openStay on the Scaredy page until they have it. Closing it, losing internet or letting your computer sleep makes the link vanish. On a phone, keep the screen on and don't switch apps.
  6. They tap ReceiveThey open the link and tap Receive all, or Receive next to a single file. Watch each one tiptoe across and save to their device. Made it. Nothing left behind.

If something goes wrong

😬 "Couldn't sneak through"Their network or yours blocks direct connections. Make a new link and choose Encrypted relay.
👻 "This link is already a ghost"The sender's page closed. Ask them to open Scaredy again and send a new link.
⏸️ It stopped partwayA phone probably paused the sender's page. Ask them to come back to the page, then open the link again to start over.
🔑 "That key doesn't fit"Part of the link got cut off. Copy the whole link again, including everything after the "#".

Good to know

Do both of us need to be online?

Yes. The file comes from the sender's open page, so it only works while that page is open. Pick a time when you're both around.

Can I send to more than one person?

Yes. Send the same link to each person. Everyone gets their own copy while your page stays open, and you'll see each person's progress.

Which option should I pick?

Start with Direct, the most private. If it says it couldn't connect, or you don't want the other person to see your internet address, use Encrypted relay. Use Short link when you need to type or say the link out loud.
